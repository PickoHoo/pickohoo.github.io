The Toolkit Regret Test: which buying mistake would you make?

Buying a plumbing tool kit? Pick the answer that sounds most like you. Hoo the owl will show you what to check. These are common buying pitfalls, not survey results.

The piece-count trap

A bigger number doesn't mean the right tools. List the plumbing jobs you actually do (radiator bleeding, tap washers, compression fittings…) and check the retailer's included-item list against them.

The storage surprise

A kit that doesn't fit your van, drawer or under-sink space gets left behind. Check the case dimensions with the retailer and picture where it will actually go.

The checkout creep
