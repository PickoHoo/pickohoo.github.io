Three checks before you choose

  1. Ask what he fixes: household jobs, woodwork or car maintenance call for different tools.
  2. For power tools, check which battery platform he already owns before buying a new kit.
  3. Compare what is included, storage, delivery costs, returns and final retailer price.

These are buying considerations, not hands-on product reviews. Tool availability, delivered price and returns are set by the retailer.
