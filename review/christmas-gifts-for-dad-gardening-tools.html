Christmas gifts for dad who loves gardening: choose by his garden

A gift for someone who already gardens is more useful when it matches the space and the work they enjoy. Check the tools they own before exploring a wider garden range.

Three checks before you choose

  1. Does he mainly plant, prune, grow vegetables or care for a lawn? Match the tool to the activity.
  2. For a small garden or containers, compact hand tools can be more practical than bulky machinery.
  3. Check materials, exact contents, storage, delivery and returns. Retailers confirm final prices.

These are buying considerations, not hands-on product reviews. Tool availability, delivered price and returns are set by the retailer.
