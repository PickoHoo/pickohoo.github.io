About PickoHoo

Clever finds. Better buys.

Shopping is noisy. PickoHoo is a UK-based shopping discovery project built around one simple idea: finding something useful should be enjoyable, and choosing it should feel clear. Picko, our curious little owl, explores gifts, home essentials, gadgets, tools and everyday discoveries.

Our selection is deliberately small while we prove the approach. Some links are affiliate links: if you buy after following one, PickoHoo may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Retailers set prices and handle payment, stock, delivery and returns.

How Picko chooses

PickoHoo is a discovery publisher, not a manufacturer, a retailer or a testing lab. We don't claim to have bought or tested products unless that's true and stated.

  1. We start with who a product is for and the practical need it meets.
  2. We use the retailer's own product data (model, price, stock and UK delivery) from its official affiliate feed, and drop anything that's out of stock or missing a working tracked link.
  3. Every price is a dated snapshot, not a checkout guarantee, and we show when it was last updated.
  4. Where information is missing (contents, delivery cost, returns), we say so rather than guess.
  5. We never invent test results, reviews, discounts, "was" prices or scarcity. We only describe a saving if we can substantiate a same-product price comparison. Black Friday alone isn't proof of a saving.
