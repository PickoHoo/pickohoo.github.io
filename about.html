How we pick

Every morning a script reads the retailer's official affiliate feed and offers. A product appears only if it is in stock, shown in a recently imported retailer feed, in season and has a working tracked link. We show at most 3. We only say "offer" when the retailer has published one, and we never show a "was" price we can't prove.

We earn a commission from the retailer if you buy through a marked link. That never changes what we show. If something looks wrong, email trypickora@gmail.com.
