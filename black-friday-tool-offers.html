Three checks before you choose

  1. Match the exact model and kit contents. A body-only tool may need a battery and charger sold separately.
  2. Check the battery platform, accessories and tools you already own before adding another kit.
  3. Confirm the retailer’s current price, stock, delivery and offer conditions. Selected offers can end or sell out.

These are buying considerations, not hands-on product reviews. Tool availability, delivered price and returns are set by the retailer.
